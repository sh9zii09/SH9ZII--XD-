01 // WHAT IS ETHICAL HACKING?

Ethical Hacking (White Hat) is the authorized practice of testing systems to find weaknesses before criminals do.

Goal is simple: improve security, protect data, and make the digital world safer. It is legal only when done with proper permission.

WHITE HAT

Authorized • Legal • Defensive
Works with permission to secure systems.

GREY HAT

Sometimes crosses lines. Finds bugs without permission but usually reports them.

BLACK HAT

Illegal • Malicious • Criminal
Exploits systems for gain or damage. NEVER do this.

02 // PHASES OF ETHICAL HACKING

01

RECONNAISSANCE

Gathering information about the target. Passive or active. Understanding the surface.

02

SCANNING

Finding open ports, services and live systems. Mapping the attack surface.

03

GAINING ACCESS

Exploiting vulnerabilities in a controlled & authorized environment only.

04

MAINTAINING ACCESS

Understanding how attackers stay inside — so defenders can detect & remove them.

05

COVERING TRACKS

Learning how evidence is hidden — so security teams can still catch it.

06

REPORTING

Documenting every finding and giving clear recommendations. Most important phase.
